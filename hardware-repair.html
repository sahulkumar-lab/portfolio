Hardware Repair

Skill

Overview

Hardware repair involves diagnosing and fixing physical issues with desktops, laptops, printers, and other peripherals, with the goal of minimizing downtime for end users.

What this involves

  • Diagnosing hardware failures such as faulty RAM, hard drives, power supplies, and motherboards.
  • Replacing and upgrading components like RAM, storage drives, and batteries.
  • Troubleshooting and repairing printer issues, including paper jams, connectivity, and driver problems.
  • Performing routine maintenance such as cleaning, cable management, and thermal paste replacement.
  • Setting up new hardware, including imaging and initial configuration for new joiners.
  • Coordinating with vendors for warranty repairs and replacement parts.
