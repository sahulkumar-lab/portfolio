Networking

Skill

Overview

Networking covers the setup, maintenance, and troubleshooting of the connections that let computers, printers, and other devices communicate with each other and with the internet.

What this involves

  • Diagnosing and resolving LAN/WAN connectivity issues for end users.
  • Configuring IP addresses, DNS, and DHCP settings on client machines.
  • Basic configuration and troubleshooting of routers, switches, and Wi-Fi access points.
  • Using tools like ping, tracert, and ipconfig to identify and isolate network problems.
  • Setting up and troubleshooting VPN connections for remote users.
  • Managing network drive mappings and shared folder permissions.
  • Coordinating with network or infrastructure teams to escalate and resolve larger issues.
