ServiceNow

Skill

Overview

ServiceNow is a cloud-based IT Service Management (ITSM) platform used to log, track, and resolve support tickets, and to manage broader IT workflows across an organization.

What this involves

  • Logging and categorizing incidents, service requests, and problems raised by end users.
  • Prioritizing tickets based on impact and urgency, and routing them to the right team.
  • Updating ticket status, adding resolution notes, and closing tickets after confirming the fix with the user.
  • Tracking Service Level Agreements (SLAs) to ensure issues are resolved within target timeframes.
  • Using the built-in knowledge base to find and apply standard fixes for common issues.
  • Generating basic reports on ticket volume, categories, and resolution times.
